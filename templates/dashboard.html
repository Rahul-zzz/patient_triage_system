Priority logic

Critical: emergency-type symptoms are assigned priority 3.

Moderate: symptoms such as fever, abdominal pain and fracture are assigned priority 2.

Low: symptoms such as headache, cold and body pain are assigned priority 1.

These rules reproduce the logic in the uploaded application and are not a medical decision tool.
